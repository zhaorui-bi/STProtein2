# Fixed foundation and the next research step

The clean baseline is reference-fitted RNA Ridge plus the already effective physical B0 at alpha0.5. The data contract, gene transform, source CV, 31-channel target, graph, spatial centering and source-macro benchmark endpoint remain fixed. The baseline uses no learned neural components. It provides a small, auditable starting point, not an algorithmic innovation claim.

The existing 198-case, three-seed structure study and complete own/neighbor seed2025 screen support retaining B0 as the reference. Completing the 16 missing target/lr cases prevents discarding complex heads solely because their optimization diagnostic was incomplete. The new scope also includes all 36 seed2025 graph controls. The remaining 339 pending cases are explicitly deferred, not passed. History, all failures and source identities remain in `/fs04/scratch2/nd32/zhaorui/STProtein/STProtein2`.

Core completion is a development gate. It does not supply the missing multi-seed selected comparisons, capacity controls, external baselines, external confirmation or biological evidence. The old four-grid/708-case completion conditions are preserved in their original protocols and are not rewritten to pass.

Next add one mechanism at a time. First test an RNA main-effect residual against ordinary neighborhood residuals with matched capacity, optimization and reference selection. A new neighborhood increment must beat both B0 and own-RNA controls across sources. Only then consider protein-specific spatial scales. Frozen model/competitor configurations precede final external RNA-only prediction and separate ADT scoring. This branch does not automatically choose a winner from the outer development scores.

The runtime intentionally omits the existing neural research components. Those controls remain reproducible in the historical checkout, and a claim requiring one of the deferred controls must restore a targeted, registered comparison.
