# Fixed foundation and the next research step

The clean baseline is reference-fitted RNA Ridge plus the already effective physical B0 at alpha0.5. The data contract, gene transform, source CV, 31-channel target, graph, spatial centering and source-macro benchmark endpoint remain fixed. The baseline uses no learned neural components. It provides a small, auditable starting point, not an algorithmic innovation claim.

The existing 198-case, three-seed structure study and complete own/neighbor seed2025 screen support retaining B0 as the reference. Completing the 16 missing target/lr cases prevents discarding complex heads solely because their optimization diagnostic was incomplete. The new scope also includes all 36 seed2025 graph controls. The remaining 339 pending cases are explicitly deferred, not passed. History, all failures and source identities remain in `/fs04/scratch2/nd32/zhaorui/STProtein/STProtein2`.

Core completion is a development gate. It does not supply the missing multi-seed selected comparisons, capacity controls, external baselines, external confirmation or biological evidence. The old four-grid/708-case completion conditions are preserved in their original protocols and are not rewritten to pass.

The completed registered three-seed comparison found the lowest mean RMSE with the own-RNA residual, but its gain was smaller than one optimizer standard error, with improvements in three of six sources. Its predeclared parsimony rule retained B0 as the compact development default. [The frozen decision](COMPACT_BASE_20261026.md) records both results and all source-level negatives.

Next add one mechanism at a time. A new neighborhood increment must be tested against B0 and own-RNA controls with matched capacity, optimization and reference selection. Protein-specific global spatial-strength fitting already exists in the research checkout and must be treated as prior project work. Literature comparison and a distinct falsifiable mechanism precede a new novelty claim. Frozen model/competitor configurations precede final external RNA-only prediction and separate ADT scoring.

The compact runtime retains only the two ordinary residual heads required for the active confirmation. Other research components remain reproducible in the historical checkout, and a claim requiring one of the deferred controls must restore a targeted, registered comparison. Converting an existing checkpoint preserves its weights; it does not establish fresh training parity or final model selection.
